# PESTLE analysis

Scans the macro-environment for forces that could shape strategy, market entry, or viability. Use early in strategy work to avoid blind spots.

## Inputs
- Industry, geography/region, and the strategic question (e.g. market entry). Read attached material; research recent regulatory/market changes if needed.

## Load company context
Read `./company-context/00-company.md` and `04-market-competitors.md` (market, region, category).

## Method
For each factor, identify 3-5 relevant items:
- Political — policy, tariffs, stability, subsidies, licensing.
- Economic — growth, rates/inflation, FX, spending, labor costs, access to capital.
- Social — demographics, cultural values, behaviors, digital adoption.
- Technological — emerging tech, automation, security/privacy needs, adoption rates.
- Legal — data/privacy law, employment, IP, consumer protection, sector regulation.
- Environmental — climate/ESG, resource scarcity, sustainability standards.
Then rate each Impact × Probability, prioritize, and turn the top factors into responses: opportunities to leverage, threats to mitigate, compliance to plan. Note leading indicators to monitor.

## Output
Save to `./outputs/<YYYY-MM-DD>_pestle_<slug>.md`: factors by category with Impact/Probability, prioritized list, and strategic responses + indicators to watch.

## Avoid
- A flat list with no impact/probability rating or response.
- Ignoring that some factors span categories (regulation = legal + political + economic).
