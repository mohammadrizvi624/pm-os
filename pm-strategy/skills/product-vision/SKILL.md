# Product vision

A product vision answers: how can we inspire people, what are we aspiring to achieve, and what values do we uphold? It should make people *feel* something, not just understand the direction — and it evolves with strategy rather than being a one-time exercise.

## Inputs
- The product or company the vision is for, and any current direction to build on. Read attached company/product material first.

## Load company context
Read `./company-context/00-company.md` (mission, values, theme) and `01-product.md`.

## Method
1. Identify the core problem being solved and who it's for.
2. Envision the ideal future state for customers and the company.
3. Draft 3-5 vision options that are each: inspiring (worth waking up for), achievable (credible given resources/market), and emotional (creates meaning).
4. Select the strongest, explain why, and show how it aligns with `00-company.md` values and the market opportunity.

## Output
Save to `./outputs/<YYYY-MM-DD>_product-vision_<slug>.md`:
```
Vision (one sentence): <memorable, jargon-free>
Why it's right: <inspiring / achievable / emotional rationale>
Alignment: <to company mission & values>
Alternatives considered: <2-4 other options>
```

## Avoid
- Jargon and abstraction — use clear, emotionally resonant language.
- Ambition with no credibility, or credibility with no ambition.
