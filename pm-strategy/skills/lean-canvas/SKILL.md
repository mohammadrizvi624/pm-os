# Lean Canvas

Captures the business hypothesis for a new product (Ash Maurya), trading completeness for speed. Use it to surface and test the riskiest assumptions; pair it with `product-strategy` when you need strategic clarity, not just a model.

## Inputs
- The product/feature, target segment, and problem space. Read attached material first.

## Load company context
Read `./company-context/01-product.md`, `03-users-personas.md`, `00-company.md`.

## Method — the 9 sections
1. Problem — top 3 customer problems + current unsatisfactory solutions.
2. Solution — top 3 features/approaches.
3. Unique value proposition — concise, memorable; why us, not just "better."
4. Unfair advantage — defensibility competitors can't easily copy.
5. Customer segments — target + early adopters.
6. Channels — how you reach them.
7. Revenue streams — model, pricing, LTV.
8. Cost structure — fixed/variable, CAC.
9. Key metrics — activation, retention, revenue (the few that matter).
Then surface the riskiest assumptions and suggest validation experiments (landing page, interviews, MVP).

## Output
Save to `./outputs/<YYYY-MM-DD>_lean-canvas_<slug>.md` as the nine sections, ending with: Riskiest assumptions · Validation experiments.

## Avoid
- Treating it as a strategy document — it's a hypothesis tool; revisit as you learn.
- Confusing Problem with Segments, or Solution with UVP — keep each section distinct.
