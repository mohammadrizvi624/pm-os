# SWOT analysis

Assesses internal factors (strengths, weaknesses) and external factors (opportunities, threats), then cross-references them into actions. Internal → external.

## Inputs
- The product/business being assessed and its competitive context. Read attached material first.

## Load company context
Read `./company-context/00-company.md`, `01-product.md`, `04-market-competitors.md`.

## Method
1. Strengths (internal, +): capabilities, IP, brand, relationships, cost/efficiency, team, distribution. 5-7.
2. Weaknesses (internal, −): resource/skill gaps, awareness, churn, cost structure, legacy, key-person risk. 5-7, honest.
3. Opportunities (external, +): growing segments, tech shifts, regulatory tailwinds, competitor gaps, adjacencies. 5-7.
4. Threats (external, −): competitors, shifting preferences, disruption, regulation, downturns. 5-7.
5. Cross-reference into strategies: S×O (leverage), W×T (defend), W×O (fix to capture), S×T (defend the flank).
6. Produce 3-5 prioritized recommendations with owners and metrics.

## Output
Save to `./outputs/<YYYY-MM-DD>_swot_<slug>.md`: the four quadrants, the cross-strategies (build / defend / pivot / exit), and prioritized recommendations.

## Avoid
- Minimizing weaknesses or listing only flattering strengths.
- Stopping at the four lists — the value is in the cross-strategies.
