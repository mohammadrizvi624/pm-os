# Competitor analysis

Maps the competitive landscape and turns it into differentiation and positioning opportunities — distinct from Porter's *industry-structure* analysis (that's `pm-strategy`).

## Inputs
- The product and market/segment. Use web research to identify competitors and gather current data (pricing pages, reviews, launches); read any attached competitor data/feature comparisons first.

## Load company context
Read `./company-context/04-market-competitors.md` (known competitors, our differentiation), `01-product.md`, `00-company.md`.

## Method
1. Scope the market and addressable customers.
2. Identify ~5 primary direct competitors (note adjacent/indirect ones too); position them as leaders / challengers / niche.
3. For each: profile (focus, segments, stage/funding), strengths, weaknesses/gaps, business model & pricing, recent moves, and how they threaten us.
4. Map differentiation opportunities: unmet needs across the set, underserved segments, JTBD not solved well, pricing/UX/channel gaps.
5. Recommend a competitive position and the threats to monitor (12-18 months).

## Output
Save to `./outputs/<YYYY-MM-DD>_competitor-analysis_<slug>.md`:
```
Market overview: <size, segments, success factors, intensity>
Competitive set: <5 competitors + positions>
Per competitor: profile · strengths · weaknesses/gaps · model & pricing · threat
Differentiation opportunities: <gaps & underserved needs>
Positioning recommendation: <our position, differentiators, threats to watch>
```

## Avoid
- Conflating direct competitors with adjacent alternatives — label both.
- Unsourced claims; validate across multiple current sources.
