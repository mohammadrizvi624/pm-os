# Product naming

Generates distinctive name options aligned to brand and audience, each with the reasoning to choose between them.

## Inputs
- The product/feature, its value, target audience, and any naming conventions or markets/languages to consider. Read attached files first.

## Load company context
Read `./company-context/00-company.md` (brand values), `03-users-personas.md`, and `08-voice-tone.md`.

## Method
Generate ~5 candidate names. For each provide:
1. **Name**.
2. **Rationale** — how it reflects the value and appeals to the audience.
3. **Brand fit** — how it supports the brand architecture and voice.
4. **Memorability** — why it's distinctive, easy to say/spell, differentiated.
5. **Trademark/domain note** — a flag on likely availability concerns (note this is a preliminary signal, not legal clearance).
Favor names that are easy to pronounce/spell, distinctive, on-brand, and relevant to the core use case.

## Output
Save to `./outputs/<YYYY-MM-DD>_product-names_<slug>.md`: the ~5 candidates with the five fields each, and a recommendation.

## Avoid
- Hard-to-spell or easily-confused names; names that collide with a known competitor.
- Presenting trademark/domain notes as legal clearance — recommend a proper search before committing.
