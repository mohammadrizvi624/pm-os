# Sales enablement

Produces concise, scannable material a rep can use mid-call — most often a competitive battlecard against a named rival, plus the objection handling around it.

## Inputs
- Your product and the specific competitor (or deal context). Use web research for the competitor's current product, pricing, and reviews (G2/Capterra/Reddit); read any win/loss or sales-call notes provided.

## Load company context
Read `./company-context/04-market-competitors.md`, `01-product.md`, and `08-voice-tone.md`.

## Method
1. Research the competitor: offerings, pricing/model, positioning, recent moves, strengths/weaknesses, customer sentiment.
2. Build the battlecard, kept scannable (tables, bold, short bullets):
   - **Overview** — their positioning in a sentence; their ICP.
   - **Quick comparison** — capability-by-capability (Us / Them / Winner).
   - **Where we win** (with proof points) and **where they win** (with our counter-positioning).
   - **Objections & responses** — "they have X", "they're cheaper", "they're more established".
   - **Landmines** — questions to plant that expose their weaknesses.
   - **Win/loss patterns** — when we win, when we lose, the deciding differentiator.

## Output
Save to `./outputs/<YYYY-MM-DD>_battlecard_<competitor>.md`, formatted for quick reference (Notion/Confluence/print).

## Avoid
- Feature dumps — reps need the few points that win the deal.
- Trashing the competitor; use honest counter-positioning (acknowledge where they win).
