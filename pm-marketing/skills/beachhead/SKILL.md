# Beachhead segment

Finds the smallest winnable, referenceable segment to dominate first — fast PMF validation and a springboard to adjacent markets (Geoffrey Moore, *Crossing the Chasm*).

## Inputs
- The product, candidate segments, and any validation data. Read attached files first.

## Load company context
Read `./company-context/04-market-competitors.md`, `03-users-personas.md`, and `00-company.md`.

## Method
1. List candidate segments (vertical, company size, role, geography, use case).
2. Score each on the four criteria:
   - **Burning pain** — acute, frequent, worsening; expensive/fragile workarounds.
   - **Willingness to pay** — budget exists, ROI clear, decision-maker has authority.
   - **Winnable share** — realistically capture ~60-70% in 3-18 months (large enough, not saturated, you have an edge or distribution advantage).
   - **Referral potential** — tight community, word-of-mouth, network effects, adjacency to expansion segments.
3. Select the highest combined score that's achievable with current resources and references into adjacent markets.
4. Sketch a 90-day acquisition plan for the beachhead and the post-beachhead expansion path.

## Output
Save to `./outputs/<YYYY-MM-DD>_beachhead_<slug>.md`:
```
Candidates scored (4 criteria) → recommended beachhead
Evidence: pain · WTP · winnable share · referral pathways
90-day acquisition plan · adjacent-expansion roadmap
```

## Avoid
- A vague mass market — start absurdly specific; a niche beats "everyone".
- Diluting across several segments; concentrate resources on dominating one.
