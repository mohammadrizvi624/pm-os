# GTM strategy

Produces the plan to take a product to market: which channels, what message, what success looks like, and the launch timeline — pulling its parts from the other marketing skills.

## Inputs
- The product, target segment, launch timeline, and constraints. Read attached files first.

## Load company context
Read `./company-context/00-company.md`, `01-product.md`, `04-market-competitors.md`, `05-metrics-goals.md`, `08-voice-tone.md`.

## Method
1. Confirm the target segment (reuse `icp`/`beachhead`) and the launch objective.
2. **Channels**: select the launch channels — see `acquisition-channels` for the motion framework; pick the few to execute excellently rather than many poorly.
3. **Messaging**: pull the launch message from `messaging`/`positioning`; add channel-specific variants and proof points.
4. **Success metrics**: define awareness → engagement → conversion → revenue KPIs with baseline and targets.
5. **Launch plan**: phased timeline — pre-launch prep, launch-day activities, post-launch momentum — with go/no-go criteria and an optimization cadence.

## Output
Save to `./outputs/<YYYY-MM-DD>_gtm-strategy_<slug>.md`:
```
Target segment & objective
Channels (chosen + why) · channel-specific messaging
Success metrics (awareness → revenue, with targets)
Launch plan: pre-launch · launch day · post-launch · go/no-go
90-day execution roadmap
```

## Avoid
- Spreading thin across many channels — focus on a few done well.
- Launching without baseline metrics to measure impact against.
